## `I.` Research Universe

I explore the intersection of **artificial intelligence, computer science, mathematics, physics, and geospatial intelligence**.

My interests span intelligent systems and their underlying computational foundations — from neural networks and high-performance computing to numerical simulation, spatial algorithms, and real-world environmental modeling.

Rather than treating these disciplines as isolated domains, I am interested in how they connect:

- How can AI make scientific computing more intelligent?
- How can mathematical models describe complex physical systems?
- How can geospatial data reveal patterns in the real world?
- How can software architecture turn research and algorithms into practical tools?
